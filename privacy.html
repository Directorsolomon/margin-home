Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

Margin is made and sold by Directorsolomon Films, Nigeria. Lemon Squeezy is the reseller (merchant of record) for every purchase.

Margin is built so that your scripts stay yours. We don't run servers that receive your scripts, and Margin has no accounts, analytics or tracking.

Your scripts

Scripts, notes and settings are stored on your computer. Writing together on a network sends the script directly between the computers in the session.

What Margin sends, and when

  • License activation: your license key and a label for the computer (such as "Margin on Windows · 7f3a") go to Lemon Squeezy when you activate, deactivate, or once a week to confirm the key.
  • Update checks: once a day Margin downloads a small file from GitHub listing the latest version. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • AI features (optional): when you ask, the script and your question go to the AI provider you chose, with your API key, under that provider's terms.
  • Neural voices (optional): when you use them, the lines being read go to Microsoft Azure AI Speech with your key.

API keys are kept in your system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain).

Purchases

Lemon Squeezy sells Margin as its reseller and handles payment details, tax and receipts under its privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details to provide your license and support.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may log visits as described in its privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
