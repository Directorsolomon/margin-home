Help with Margin

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, report a problem or ask a question on GitHub.

Where is my license key?

In the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy. If you can't find it, look up your order at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders with the email you paid with.

How do I activate Margin?

Choose File ▸ Margin license…, paste your key and choose Activate. Activating needs an internet connection once; after that Margin works offline.

How do I move Margin to a new computer?

On the old computer, open File ▸ Margin license… and choose Deactivate this computer. Then activate the key on the new one. A key works on up to 3 computers at a time.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your scripts still open, read and export, and nothing is deleted. Editing needs a license key.

Does Margin send my script anywhere?

No. Scripts stay on your computer. The optional AI features send the script only when you ask, only to the provider you choose, with your own API key. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. Writing, the story model and the offline notes need no AI at all. To use the AI reading and notes, bring a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter or Mistral, or run a model locally with Ollama.

How do updates work?

Margin checks once a day, shows a small banner when a new version is ready and installs it when you choose Restart to update. It waits until your script is saved. Updates are free forever.

Windows or macOS warns me when I open Margin

Margin is new and not yet recognised by Windows SmartScreen or Apple. The download page shows the one-time step to open it.
