Download Margin

Free for 14 days with everything on. No account and no card. Margin updates itself when a new version is out.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Download for Windows
  1. Open Margin-Windows-setup.exe.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. Margin is new, so Windows doesn't know it yet.
  3. Margin installs for your account only; no administrator needed.

Mac

macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Download for Mac
  1. Open Margin-macOS.dmg and drag Margin to Applications.
  2. Open Margin. If macOS says it can't check it for malicious software, open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. You only need to do this once.

After 14 days

Your scripts still open, read and export. To keep writing, buy Margin and enter your license key in File ▸ Margin license….

Updates

Margin checks for a new version once a day and asks before it restarts to install it. You can also choose File ▸ Check for updates…. Every version is listed in the changelog, and older installers are on GitHub.
