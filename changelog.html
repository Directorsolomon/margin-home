What's new in Margin

Every version, newest first. Installers for each one are on GitHub.

0.2.0

November 2026
  • Margin for Mac: Cmd shortcuts, a Mac menu bar, and scripts that open from Finder.
  • Updates: Margin checks once a day and installs new versions when you restart.
  • A 14-day free trial, and license keys to keep writing after it.
  • Choose the AI model for readings and notes: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Ollama or any compatible service.
  • Notes remember why you set some aside, and show which checks you find least useful.

0.1.0

October 2026
  • The first version: screenplay formatting, SmartType, import and export, the story model, ranked notes, production tools and writing together on a network.
